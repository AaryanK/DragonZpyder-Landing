# DragonZpyder — Harness all of AI

**Mission:** Connect intelligence, tools and AI services so everyone can access useful capabilities without depending on a single model, interface or isolated subscription.

## Vision

- **AI Mesh (planned):** one user interface for choosing AI models, orchestrating specialist models, connecting creative tools and tracking usage costs. An initial gateway could use infrastructure such as OpenRouter.
- **Persistent intelligence (long-term):** continuity of identity, memory, permissions and task context across model changes and devices.
- **Project IC (research concept):** a shared accounting and credit layer. Participating applications could offer limited-use experiences, usage pricing and partner settlement instead of requiring a separate subscription each time. Existing credits at unrelated platforms do not automatically transfer.
- **Compute economy (future research):** investigate GPU-hours, FLOPs, verifiable useful work and contractual compute-service redemption. Neither FLOPs nor GPU-hours directly measure intelligence, and neither guarantees stable currency value.

## Example future workflow

A person chooses a primary reasoning model, grants permission to delegate code, research, image, sound or video tasks to specialist models, reviews the resulting work and pays against one transparent budget. Excellent results still require creative direction, asset continuity and editing.

## Roadmap

1. Prototype the AI mesh: model switching, usage metering, spending limits and bounded multi-model orchestration.
2. Pilot an auditable conventional ledger and user experience; test real demand and unit economics.
3. Explore a compliant partner SDK and settlement network with consenting services, separating monetary balances from promotional and earned value.
4. Research compute-service markets and possible resource-backed instruments without assuming a tradeable token is needed.

## Principles

Human benefit, portability, informed consent, transparent pricing, trustworthy security, and genuine interoperability. The name evokes fire as creative energy and the image of a spider that spins a web without being confined by it—drawing inspiration from Indic philosophy while welcoming people of all beliefs and backgrounds.

## Current status

This repository is a **static landing page**. The AI Mesh, universal wallet, Project IC credits, partner network, and compute-backed currency are **plans or research directions, not launched services**. Any real monetary wallet, partner settlements, redemption or tokenisation require careful regulatory, reserve, payment-processor and platform design.
